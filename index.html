⚠️ Important Reminders
◆AHCC — 3g as a single morning dose on an empty stomach, at least 30 min before food or other supplements. This matches the clinical trial protocol — don't split it
◆Zinc — Once daily at breakfast (25mg) + 15mg from Calcium AKG = 40mg/day total, which is at the upper limit. If continuing past 3 months, check copper levels or add 1mg copper
◆Ashwagandha — Standalone removed. Saffron+ Advanced still contains some KSM-66, which is the single source now
◆Fat-soluble supplements — Omega-3, D3+K2, Beta-Carotene, and Turmeric Curcumin must be taken with a meal containing healthy fats
◆Blood work — Get baseline zinc + CBC levels before starting, recheck at 6 weeks
Always consult your physician before starting any new supplement protocol · This schedule is for wellness support only
